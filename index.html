HVAC Terminology, Tips & Hints

A few terms worth knowing before you buy — and videos that show how your systems actually work.

  • SEER — Seasonal Energy Efficiency Ratio. The higher the SEER rating, the less electricity an air conditioner uses to cool your home.
  • AFUE — Annual Fuel Utilization Efficiency. A furnace's AFUE percentage tells you how much of its fuel becomes heat for your home.
  • BTU — British Thermal Unit. A measure of heating and cooling capacity — the right size matters more than the biggest size.
  • ERV / HRV — Energy / Heat Recovery Ventilators. They exchange stale indoor air for fresh outdoor air while recovering energy from the outgoing air.
  • Tip: Change or clean your furnace filter regularly — a clogged filter makes your system work harder and can shorten its life.
  • Tip: Keep indoor humidity between 30% and 60% for comfort, healthier air and protection for your home's woodwork.
